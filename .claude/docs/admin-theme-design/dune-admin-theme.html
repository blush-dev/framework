Palette

Warmth lives in the grays, the sand; coolness lives in the accent, the shadow. That warm-cool pairing is what keeps it from reading as a beige Neutral.

Install Check

The contrast pairs from the theming spec, measured live. Errors need 4.5:1 (3:1 for muted ink); dots are warnings at 3:1.

Open Questions

  1. Sand without cream. The grounds are kept grayish so they read as sand rather than the warm-paper look of Editorial. Warm enough, or too warm?
  2. Indigo versus Neutral's cobalt. The accent leans violet and much deeper. In dark it is a moonlit periwinkle, which is the closest any sketch has come to Neutral. Keep it, or push it toward violet?
  3. Corners. 7 / 12 / 18px, a little softer than Neutral, like eroded edges. Keep, or stay with Neutral's?
  4. Name and id. "Dune" is a working name.

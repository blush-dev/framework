# Activity

With `site.activity` (owners, administrators, and editors),
**Tools → Activity** lists every change to entries and media, newest
first.

Each row says what happened, which opens the change's own screen (what
it changed, before and after), who did it, how (in the admin, by a
connected app, named, on the command line, or through the API), and
when, with **Undo** where it can be undone, or why it can't. Filter by
who, how, and what changed. See [AI apps and MCP](../features/mcp/README.md)
for how activity and Undo work.

An entry's own changes are also on the **Activity** row at the end of
the editor's [Document tab](editor/document-tab.md#rows-at-the-end-of-the-document-tab).
